# About Jossyglamour

**Jossyglamour** is a UK-based beauty and fashion brand in **Newcastle, United Kingdom**. The brand is led by founder and creative director **Josephine Chukwu** and specialises in custom headwear, fascinators, professional makeup, and personal styling for weddings, cultural events, photoshoots, and special occasions.

*Endless Beauty Without Borders.*

---

## Who We Are

Jossyglamour designs and creates unique headpieces and accessories, including fascinators, hats, turbans, gele (African headwrap), customised hand fans, and traditional caps. We also offer professional makeup, hair, and beauty services, along with one-on-one styling so every client looks and feels confident on their big day.

## Our Founder

Josephine Chukwu has worked in the beauty and fashion industry since 2019. Her work combines makeup artistry, photography, and bespoke fashion accessories. Each piece is made to complement the client's style, outfit, and occasion, whether that is a wedding, fashion event, celebration, or formal gathering.

## Our Vision

We aim to grow Jossyglamour into a fashion and creative business that offers training, production, and skills development to empower others.
